Why's That? — Guide for parents

Everything you need to know, in about five minutes.

Why's That? is a simple website where children can ask whatever they're wondering about — out loud or typed — and get a calm, clear answer read back to them. No login, no subscription, no data leaving their device. You set it up once, and then they can use it on their own.

Before you start — getting an API key

Why's That? runs on Claude, Anthropic's AI. To use it, you'll need your own API key — a private code that connects the app to your Anthropic account. It takes about three minutes to get one, and the answers are billed directly to you at a very low cost (more on that below).

2

What your child sees

After setup, the app goes straight to a single microphone button. They tap it, ask their question out loud, and the mic stays open — it won't cut them off if they pause to think. When they've finished, the app finds the answer and reads it back in plain language: three to five sentences, no jargon, with a comparison to something they already know.

At the end of each answer, there's a short hands-on activity they can try right now — usually something involving water, salt, paper, or a torch (things already in the house). Early readers who can't yet type can use it completely independently, since the whole experience is voice-in and voice-out.

If they'd rather type than speak, there's a quiet "Type instead" option below the mic. And if the same question comes up again, the answer is cached — so it loads instantly and doesn't use any more credits.

3

The parent view

At the bottom of the home screen there's a small "For parents →" link. Open it, and you'll see a log of every question your child has asked, grouped by date — the question, the answer they were given, and the activity suggested.

Generate lesson ideas

There's a button that sends the full question log to Claude and asks it to suggest three to five lesson ideas or activities based on what your child has actually been curious about. It notes the age range each activity suits, roughly how long it takes, and which of their questions it connects to. You can copy the suggestions or email them to yourself.

Worth knowing

The question log is saved in your browser's local storage — which means it lives on the specific device and browser they use the app on. If they switch to a different tablet, or if the browser storage gets cleared, the history starts fresh. There's no cloud account, which is also why there's no signup. The parent view works best when there's one dedicated device they always use it on (and probably sounds like more of a constraint than it is in practice — most kids have a preferred device they return to).

That's everything. The setup takes a few minutes; after that it mostly runs itself. Questions your child asks this week might surprise you — and probably point to a lesson you wouldn't have thought to plan.

How it works
🗓️ Every morning — the child opens the timetable and sees only that day's sessions. No jumping ahead.
▶ One tap to start — each activity card opens directly in a new tab. The timetable stays open behind it.
🏗️ Fully custom-built — Connectives and Word Explorer are bespoke resources designed to directly feed Night Zookeeper writing sessions. French Explorer, Brownie Trailhead and Historical Heroes are standalone custom resources.
🔄 English rotates — Night Zookeeper (Mon/Wed), Connectives (Tue/Fri) and Word Explorer (Thu) cycle through the week so content builds on itself.
🧁 Real-world themes — Brownie Trailhead is built around a business the child is actually running. Learning stays grounded in real life.
🔊 Text-to-speech throughout — vocabulary and story sentences are spoken aloud via a local Python server, keeping it immersive for early readers.
